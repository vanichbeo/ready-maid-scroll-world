# PPL V1.32 — Technical & Local SEO Foundation

## Scope
SEO-only update based on V1.31. No visible layout, approved imagery, colours, spacing, copy blocks, navigation, social presentation, or interactions were redesigned.

## Completed
- Search-focused homepage title and meta description
- Updated Open Graph and X/Twitter metadata
- Canonical and en-MY/x-default hreflang declarations
- Expanded EmploymentAgency / LocalBusiness JSON-LD
- Legal operator, telephone, address, licence identifiers, company registration and Malaysia service area in structured data
- Facebook and Instagram `sameAs` references; broken RedNote URL omitted
- More descriptive non-visible H1 for the image-led hero
- Updated Privacy Policy and Terms metadata/canonical URLs
- Sitemap last-modified, change-frequency and priority signals
- Cache-busting version updated to V1.32

## Deliberately not added
- Business hours, because no confirmed official hours were supplied
- RedNote `sameAs`, because the supplied link returned 404
- New service/location pages, because this version is restricted to technical SEO and must not change the approved homepage experience
- Unverified reviews, ratings, pricing or ranking claims
