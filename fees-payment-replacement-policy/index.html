External and Additional Costs

Government, immigration, embassy, medical, insurance, airfare, source-country or other third-party charges may be separate from agency service charges depending on the customer’s quotation and placement. Any amount already paid to a third party and not recoverable may affect a cancellation refund.

Replacement Support

Replacement eligibility depends on the customer’s package, signed agreement, qualifying circumstances and the stage of the placement. A replacement or candidate change is not automatically the same as a cash refund.

Payment Safety

  • Check the quotation, candidate reference and payment stage before paying.
  • Pay only to an account or payment channel confirmed by Ready Maid.
  • Obtain and retain the official receipt and payment record.
