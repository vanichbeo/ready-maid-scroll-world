Find a Filipino Domestic Helper for Your Family

Ready Maid Agency is operated by Agensi Pekerjaan Expert Sdn. Bhd. · Licence C · JTKSM 432

Ready Maid helps Malaysian families review and interview Filipino domestic-helper candidates for baby care, child care, elderly care, cooking and household support. A suitable candidate should be selected according to her actual experience, communication, attitude and compatibility with your household—not nationality alone.

When a Filipino Helper May Suit Your Family

  • English communication
  • Experience with babies or children
  • Support for children’s English-speaking routines
  • Elderly-care experience
  • Confident verbal communication and reporting
  • Cooking and general household support
  • Ability to follow structured household routines

Candidates differ in work history, communication ability, baby-care confidence, elderly-care experience, cooking, personality, expectations, emotional maturity and adaptability.

Types of Filipino Helpers

First-time overseas candidates

They may have local household, child-care, elderly-family care, cooking and cleaning experience, but limited overseas employment exposure. They may need more guidance after arrival.

Experienced overseas helpers

They may have worked in Malaysia, Singapore, Hong Kong, Taiwan, Middle Eastern countries or elsewhere. Employers should verify the household, children’s ages, exact care duties, cooking responsibilities and reason the employment ended.

Transfer candidates

A Filipino transfer candidate may already be in Malaysia and looking for a suitable new employer, subject to procedures and approvals. Families should confirm the transfer reason, current status, interview availability and expected starting date.

Baby and Child Care

A Filipino candidate may be suitable when she has relevant age-specific experience and demonstrates safe handling, hygiene, patience, feeding, bottle cleaning, diaper changing, clear communication and ability to follow parental routines.

English communication can be useful for children’s daily routines and written schedules, but English alone does not prove child-care suitability. Safety, patience, honesty and actual experience remain essential.

Elderly Care

Filipino candidates may be suitable for elderly care when they have relevant experience and can communicate sufficiently with the elderly person and family. Assess mobility support, wheelchair use, bathing and toileting, diaper changing, medication reminders, night duties and physical ability.

Do not assume a candidate is a qualified nurse unless her qualifications and role have been properly verified.

Cooking and Household Support

Cooking ability varies. Some candidates may have experience with Filipino food, simple Western dishes, Chinese-style meals, soups, stir-fries, child-friendly meals, elderly-friendly meals or basic baking. Ask candidates to name dishes and explain what they cooked independently.

Communication Assessment

During the interview, assess whether the candidate can understand household instructions, explain previous work, ask questions, report mistakes, describe problems, follow written schedules and communicate with children or elderly family members.

A confident speaker is not automatically the strongest worker. Answers should be specific, realistic, consistent with the biodata and honest about limitations.

Filipino or Indonesian Helper?

A Filipino candidate may suit a family that values English communication, candidate-specific baby or child-care experience and confident reporting. An Indonesian candidate may suit a family that values Bahasa Melayu communication, regional cooking familiarity or candidate-specific elderly-care experience. Choose the individual candidate, not the stereotype.

How Ready Maid Reviews Filipino Candidates

  • Employment history and household size
  • Baby, child and elderly-care experience
  • Cooking and English communication
  • Interview consistency
  • Candidate expectations
  • Employer requirements and desired start date
  • Overall family compatibility

Information Families Should Share

  1. Employer’s location
  2. Household members
  3. Main helper duties
  4. Desired starting date

Additional details may include children’s ages, elderly mobility, language needs, cooking requirements, house type, pets, night duties and whether English communication is essential.

Interview and Verification

Discuss previous employers, household members, main duties, daily routine, baby or child-care experience, elderly care, cooking, difficult situations, reason for leaving, duties the candidate does not wish to perform, salary, rest and candidate questions.

Verify full name, age, passport details where available, employment dates, countries worked, children’s ages, elderly condition, main duties, reason for leaving, salary expectations and availability.

First-Time or Experienced Candidate?

First-time candidates may be flexible and motivated but need more training and adjustment support. Experienced candidates may understand overseas routines and communicate more confidently, but previous experience still needs to match the new job.

Application, Fees and Support

A Filipino domestic-helper application may involve employer assessment, candidate review, interview, confirmation, employer and candidate documentation, source-country processing, authority submission, immigration approval, embassy, medical, insurance and visa requirements, exit clearance, travel and post-arrival formalities.

Current total package: RM14,999. Country-specific costs and procedures may differ. The final quotation must be revalidated.

Current cancellation structure: full refund before confirmation; RM4,000 after confirmation; RM6,000 after immigration approval; RM8,500 after ticket issuance. Current replacement support provides one replacement within the contract period, subject to signed terms.
