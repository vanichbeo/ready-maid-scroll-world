Baby Care Handover Checklist for a Domestic Helper

A clear handover helps a domestic helper understand how your household wants baby-care routines handled. Record the baby’s usual feeding, sleep, hygiene and soothing routines, explain important boundaries, show where essential items are kept, and agree on how daily updates should be shared. Keep the checklist practical and specific to your own household.

Start with the baby’s normal daily rhythm

Write down the usual order of the baby’s day, including typical feeding, nap, play and bath periods. Exact times may change, so focus on the normal sequence and the signs your family uses to decide what comes next. Walk through the routine together instead of relying only on written notes.

Explain feeding instructions clearly

Show what feeding items are used, where they are stored and how your household records feeds. Explain any preparation steps that are specific to your family and point out which instructions must be followed exactly. If anything is unclear or unusual, ask the helper to check with you rather than guess.

Show the sleep and settling routine

Describe the signs that usually mean the baby is tired and demonstrate the normal settling routine your household follows. Show the helper where sleep items are kept and which steps should stay consistent. Keep the explanation focused on your own routine rather than assuming every baby follows the same pattern.

Cover hygiene, changing and everyday care

Show where diapers, wipes, clothes, towels and other everyday baby-care items are kept. Demonstrate the order your household prefers for changing, bathing and cleaning up. Explain which items are for the baby only and what should be reported to you if supplies run low or something looks unusual.

Set clear boundaries and escalation rules

State the limits of what the helper should decide independently and when you expect to be contacted. Give examples based on your household, such as an unexpected change in routine, a missed feed, unusual crying or any situation the helper is unsure about. The safest rule is to ask rather than improvise when instructions are unclear.

Agree on a simple daily update format

Choose a short update format that both sides can follow, such as noting feeds, naps, changes, supplies and anything unusual. Keep it brief enough to use every day. Review the notes together during the first few days so misunderstandings can be corrected early and the routine can become easier to follow.

Frequently asked questions

Should the handover checklist include exact times?

Include usual times when they are helpful, but also explain the normal sequence and the baby’s cues because routines can vary from day to day.

How detailed should the baby-care checklist be?

Detailed enough to remove uncertainty, but short enough to use daily. Prioritize routines, item locations, household boundaries and when to contact the employer.

What should happen if the helper is unsure about an instruction?

The checklist should make clear that the helper should ask the employer instead of guessing when an instruction or situation is unclear.
