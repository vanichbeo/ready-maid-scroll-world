Direct answer

Choose a baby-care helper based on verified infant-care experience, hygiene awareness, safety habits, patience, communication and ability to follow the parents’ routine. Nationality, confidence and total years of employment are not enough. Ask detailed questions about the baby’s age, feeding, bottle hygiene, bathing, sleep routines, night duties, emergency reporting and whether the candidate was the main caregiver.

Start with your baby’s actual needs

Define the baby’s age, whether the mother is returning to work, whether the helper will be the main caregiver, whether night assistance is required, whether there are older children, and how much cooking and cleaning is expected.

What experience should parents look for?

Similar age experience

Ask the age of the youngest baby, how long the candidate cared for the baby, whether she was the main caregiver, and whether the mother or grandmother was present.

Actual responsibilities

Check milk preparation, bottle sterilising, diaper changing, bathing, burping, sleep routine, baby laundry, simple baby food, equipment cleaning and parent updates.

Length and recency

Consider how long the experience lasted, how recent it was, whether it was full-time, and whether the candidate can explain the routine consistently.

Seven most important selection factors

  • Safety awareness
  • Hygiene awareness
  • Patience and emotional stability
  • Ability to follow instructions
  • Communication
  • Willingness to learn
  • Family compatibility

Important baby-care interview questions

  • What was the age of the youngest baby you cared for?
  • Were you the main caregiver?
  • How did you prepare milk and clean bottles?
  • What would you do if the baby would not stop crying?
  • What would you do if the baby fell or appeared unwell?
  • Are you comfortable following a fixed routine and agreed night duties?

How to verify experience

Ask for details, ask the same topic in different ways, compare answers with the biodata and use scenario questions.

Indonesian or Filipino helper for baby care?

Neither nationality is automatically better. Focus on infant-care experience, hygiene, safety, patience, communication, willingness to follow instructions and family fit.

Should you choose an experienced mother?

Being a mother may provide useful experience but does not automatically confirm suitability. Assess actual responsibilities, safety awareness and ability to follow another family’s instructions.

Night-care expectations

Discuss frequency, parent involvement, rest during the day, sleeping arrangement and whether the arrangement is temporary or long term.

Preparing your home

Prepare a written routine, feeding instructions, emergency contacts, safe chemical storage, baby-equipment instructions, duties and priorities, and a method for daily updates.

The first two weeks

Demonstrate routines, observe safety and hygiene, correct early and increase responsibility gradually.

Warning signs

  • Claims she can do everything but cannot explain how
  • Conflicting employment details
  • Little concern for safety
  • Unwillingness to report accidents
  • Defensive response to correction
  • Unrealistic expectations about duties or rest

Frequently asked questions

Is newborn experience necessary?

It is strongly relevant when the helper will independently care for a newborn.

Is a Filipino helper better for baby care?

Not automatically.

Is an Indonesian helper suitable for a newborn?

Yes, when the candidate has relevant experience, safety awareness and ability to follow the parents’ routine.

Can the helper care for the baby and complete all housework?

This depends on the baby’s age, routine, house size and family support.
