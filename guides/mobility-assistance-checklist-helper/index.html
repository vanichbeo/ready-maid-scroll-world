Complete Household Checklist for Domestic Helper Mobility Assistance

A mobility assistance checklist helps your domestic helper systematically support a household member with limited mobility. It outlines daily tasks like transferring, dressing, and medication reminders, while emphasizing safety protocols to prevent falls and ensure comfort. This structured guide ensures consistent, compassionate care and clear communication within the household.

Assessing Home Safety and Accessibility

Begin by walking through your home with the checklist to identify potential hazards. Ensure clear pathways by removing loose rugs, cords, and clutter. Check that all rooms, especially the bathroom and bedroom, have adequate lighting and consider installing grab bars near toilets and showers. Verify that commonly used items are within easy reach to minimize unnecessary bending or stretching. This proactive assessment forms the foundation for daily assistance.

Daily Transfer and Mobility Support

When assisting with transfers, always communicate clearly. Use proper lifting techniques by bending at the knees, not the waist, and encourage the individual to assist as much as they are able. For bed-to-wheelchair moves, ensure the brakes are locked and use a transfer belt if available. Practice patience and repeat movements slowly to build confidence. Include scheduled rest periods between activities to avoid fatigue.

Assisting with Personal Care and Hygiene

Use a shower chair and handheld showerhead for safe bathing. Prepare all toiletries beforehand to prevent the helper from leaving the individual unattended. When dressing, lay out clothes in order and use adaptive aids like button hooks or elastic laces if needed. For grooming, assist with tasks like hair brushing or shaving only as required, always explaining each action. Maintain privacy and warmth throughout the process.

Medication and Appointment Management

Create a shared log or use a pill organizer to track medication times and dosages. The helper should be trained to read labels and note any refills needed. For appointments, maintain a calendar with details on transport arrangements and pre-visit preparation. The checklist should include reminders for the helper to inform you of any changes in the individual's mobility or comfort levels observed during these routines.
