What Is a JTKSM Licence C Maid Agency?

A Licence C agency is a private employment agency licensed under Malaysia’s Private Employment Agencies Act 1981 to carry out recruitment activities for Malaysian and non-Malaysian job seekers, including non-citizen employees within Malaysia. Employers should verify the legal agency name, licence number, category and validity through the official JTKSM agency register.

What is a private employment agency?

A private employment agency conducts recruitment activities between job seekers and employers and is regulated under the Private Employment Agencies Act 1981 [Act 246].

What does Licence C cover?

Licence C has the broadest recruitment scope among the three categories and may cover Malaysian job seekers within and outside Malaysia and non-citizen employees within Malaysia, including foreign domestic workers, subject to applicable laws and procedures.

Licence A, B and C

Licence categoryGeneral recruitment scope
Licence AMalaysian job seekers for employment within Malaysia
Licence BMalaysian job seekers within and outside Malaysia, and foreign domestic workers within Malaysia
Licence CMalaysian job seekers within and outside Malaysia, and non-citizen employees within Malaysia, including foreign domestic workers

How can customers verify a maid agency?

Check the legal company name, licence number, category, validity, office address, payment instructions and official documents against the JTKSM register.

Why does licence category matter?

Different categories cover different recruitment activities. Employers should not assume every employment-related company has the same authority.

Does Licence C guarantee a perfect helper?

No. It confirms licensed authority, not a perfect candidate, guaranteed approval, fixed processing time or conflict-free employment.

Why avoid unlicensed intermediaries?

Risks include wrong-party payments, misrepresented licence details, incomplete documentation, unclear refund or replacement responsibility and personal-document misuse.

Questions to ask before engaging an agency

  • What is your legal company name?
  • What is your JTKSM licence number and category?
  • When does the licence expire?
  • Where is the licensed office?
  • Who issues the quotation and receipt?
  • Who receives payment?
  • What services are included?
  • What are the cancellation and replacement terms?

Warning signs

  • Refusal to disclose legal company name
  • Licence cannot be found
  • Licence belongs to an unrelated company
  • Payment requested to an unrelated personal account
  • Claims of guaranteed government approval
  • Expired licence
  • Address mismatch
