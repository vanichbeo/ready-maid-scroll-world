HELPFUL GUIDANCE BEFORE AND AFTER HIRING

Four principles for a better start
01

Match the Household

Experience matters, but the right routines, communication style and care focus matter more.

Start with duties, family members and daily pace.
02

Interview for Real Life

Use simple situations to understand habits, judgement and willingness to learn.

Ask what she would do—not only what she has done.
03

Set Expectations Clearly

Explain priorities, house rules and rest arrangements before small issues become conflict.

Clear routines are kinder than repeated correction.
04

Support the First Weeks

Allow time to learn the home while checking progress with calm, specific feedback.

Correct one issue at a time and recognise improvement.
