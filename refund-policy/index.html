Refund & Cancellation Policy

We want customers to understand clearly what happens to payments if a placement is cancelled. This policy explains refund eligibility, cancellation charges and how a refund request is handled.

1. Who This Policy Applies To

This Refund & Cancellation Policy applies to domestic helper placement services provided by Ready Maid Agency, operated by Agensi Pekerjaan Expert Sdn. Bhd. (Company No. 200101016623 (552380-K), Licence C · JTKSM 432).

Your final quotation and signed service agreement remain the controlling documents for your individual placement. If there is a difference between this website summary and a signed agreement, the signed agreement applies, subject to applicable Malaysian law.

Important: a cancellation charge is not an additional payment on top of money already received. It is used to calculate the refundable balance from payments already made, together with any applicable non-recoverable external costs.

3. External Costs That May Not Be Refundable

Some amounts are paid to third parties or government bodies as part of the placement process. Where those amounts have already been paid and cannot be recovered, they may be deducted from the refundable balance. Examples can include:

  • government, immigration, embassy or source-country charges;
  • medical examination, insurance or statutory processing charges;
  • airfare, ticket cancellation or travel-related charges;
  • other third-party costs specifically incurred for the customer’s placement.

Any such deduction should relate to costs actually incurred for the placement. Ready Maid will explain the refund calculation when reviewing the request.

4. Candidate Change, Replacement and Refunds Are Different

A request to change a candidate or use replacement support is not automatically a cash-refund request. Replacement eligibility and related costs depend on the customer’s package, signed agreement, the stage of the application and the circumstances of the case.

Where replacement benefits apply, Ready Maid will explain whether the case is handled as a replacement, reselection, reapplication or refund.

Please contact Ready Maid through our official customer-service channel and state that you are requesting a refund or cancellation review.

  • Customer / employer name
  • Contact number
  • Candidate name or placement reference, if available
  • Receipt or payment reference
  • Reason for cancellation or refund request

6. Refund Review and Payment

Ready Maid will review the placement stage, payments received, work already completed, applicable cancellation charge, third-party costs and the signed agreement. Once a refund is approved, the approved amount and payment arrangement will be confirmed to the customer in writing.

Where practical, an approved refund will be returned to the original payer or an account verified as belonging to the customer. Banking or payment-provider processing time may vary after the refund has been released.

7. Payment Records

Customers should retain quotations, receipts, payment confirmations, candidate references and signed agreements. These records help Ready Maid verify and process a refund request accurately.

8. Consumer Rights

Nothing in this policy is intended to exclude rights or remedies that cannot lawfully be excluded under applicable Malaysian law.
