Refund & Cancellation Policy

Ready Maid uses a staged payment arrangement. If a customer cancels, the refund or non-refundable processing fee depends on the stage reached at the time of cancellation.

How the Refund Is Calculated

Where a refund is due, the applicable non-refundable processing fee shown above is deducted from the amount already paid by the customer. The remaining balance, if any, is the refundable amount.

Example: if RM4,500 has been paid and the customer cancels after profile confirmation, the RM4,000 non-refundable processing fee applies and the remaining RM500 is refundable.

Replacement Term

Replacement is limited to one time during the contract. Deposit will be retained during border closure with no extra charge.

Please contact Ready Maid through our official customer-service channel and provide the customer name, contact number, candidate or placement reference (if available), payment receipt/reference, and the cancellation request.
